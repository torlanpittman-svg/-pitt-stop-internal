**Labor cost is never recorded** on jobs, and there are no `sublet` lines in the period. Contribution profit therefore
cannot be computed from recorded data today. The report built in this project shows contribution as **MODELED**
(invoiced revenue − recorded part/sublet cost − a labor-cost assumption the owner sets as a % of labor price), labelled as
an assumption, never as a measured number. Recording actual labor/sublet cost per job is the owner decision that turns
this into a measured figure.
